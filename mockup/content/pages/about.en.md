FAR EST was founded in 1991. Until 2000 it ran one of the first networks of specialist shops, and the new factory opened in 2007 with latest-generation robots.

# Profiles

The company chose the German manufacturer **Profine** (Trocal profile) as supplier, alongside Kömmerling, Gealan and Salamander.
